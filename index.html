Free Printable

The Kindness
Passport

A playful adventure that helps children turn kindness into action.

Join Truly the Turtle on a kindness-filled journey. This free printable passport invites young explorers to complete four simple missions inspired by welcoming others, friendship, courage and caring for our world.

  • Welcome someone with a smile or hello
  • Explore and discover with a friend
  • Try something new and brave
  • Learn how to help protect animals and nature
  • Make their own Wave of Kindness promise

Perfect for families, classrooms, libraries and community groups, the Kindness Passport helps children discover that even one small act of kindness can travel farther than they imagine.
