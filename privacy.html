Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

We collect as little as we can, use it only for what you asked for, and never sell it.

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

Kindessa™ is Grace Frank's world of children's books, stories and kindness activities, and a Wisdom Production Group company. When this policy says “we” or “us”, it means Kindessa and Wisdom Production Group. You can reach us any time at grace@gracefrankbooks.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us.

  • Joining Grace's Readers. When you sign up for book news or the free Kindness Passport, we collect your first name and email address.
  • Contacting us. When you use the contact form, we collect your name, your email address and your message.
  • Visiting the site. Like every website, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These are used to keep the site secure and running, not to identify you.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels.

How we use it

  • To send you the Kindness Passport and the book news and updates you signed up for.
  • To reply to your message.
  • To keep the website safe and working properly.

We never sell, rent or trade your information.

Who helps us

Our website, email list and email delivery are hosted by Hostinger, which stores your information on our behalf and only uses it to provide those services to us. Links to our YouTube channel, Wisdom Bedtime Stories, take you to YouTube, where Google's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Children's privacy

Our stories are written for children, but our sign-up and contact forms are for parents, teachers and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us their details, please email us and we will delete them.

Your choices

  • Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing grace@gracefrankbooks.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address for as long as you stay subscribed. We keep contact messages only for as long as we need them to help you.

Keeping it safe

The site is served over a secure, encrypted connection, and access to your information is limited to the people who need it to run Kindessa.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

If you have any questions about your privacy, email grace@gracefrankbooks.com and we will be happy to help.
